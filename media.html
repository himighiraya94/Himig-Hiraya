HH Media Team

Roles & Responsibilities

The HH Media Team is the creative group behind the Himig Hiraya choir. Made up of photographers, videographers, graphic designers, website creators, and other creative members, they handle everything visual and digital for the group. They take photos during choir events and performances, create video recaps of their singing ministries, design publication materials, and keep the website and social media active. Their main goal is to share Himig Hiraya’s musical journey, highlight special moments of worship, and keep the community connected and informed.
